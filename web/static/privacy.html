What we store

When you sign in with Amazon, we keep your Amazon user id, name, and email so we can show your folders, cases, and notes. Case layouts, notes, and folder names are stored in our database, keyed to that Amazon id.

We do not sell this data. We do not show it to other users. Login with Amazon only receives the profile scope (name, email, user id).

A signed session cookie stays on your device so you stay logged in. You can sign out at any time.
